## How to use the bench

- Quote every deal with 2-3 brokers minimum: one BR local (Higginbotham or HUB),
  one habitational specialist (Alliant or Apartment Guard), rotate the third.
- Ask for carrier name + AM Best rating + admitted vs. non-admitted on every quote.
  Non-admitted = no LIGA (state guaranty fund) backstop; price accordingly.
- A percentage wind deductible (2-5% of TIV) on a $1M building is $20-50K out of
  pocket per storm — model it, don't ignore it.
- Feed the winning quote back into the acquisition model Inputs (insurance line),
  replacing the $2,000/unit placeholder.
