# Infill Manufactured-Home Economics (2026-08-09)

The "miniature cost edge": new single-section home, wholesale-delivered and set
on a pad you already own (utilities live). Incremental basis — park overhead is
already carried by existing units.

## Base case: $80K all-in, $1,300/mo, Livingston millage

- GPR $15,600 → EGR at 86% economic = $13,416
- Incremental OpEx $4,724 (mgmt 8%, ins $1,100, R&M $700, capex $400,
  util $250, G&A $200, mktg $200, tax = 10% assessment × parish mills)
- NOI **$8,692** → **10.9% yield on cost**

## Yield-on-cost matrix

| all-in cost | $1,200/mo | $1,300/mo | $1,400/mo |
|---|---|---|---|
| $70K | 11.2% | **12.6%** | 13.9% |
| $80K | 9.7% | 10.9% | 12.1% |
| $90K | 8.5% | 9.5% | 10.6% |

## The honest comparison that orders the strategy

- Eden at ask: $94.4K/door at 8.44% cap
- Eden at $1.25M ideal: $69.4K/door at **11.5% going-in cap**
- New infill home: $80K/door at 10.9%

**A well-negotiated acquisition still beats building — today.** Eden at the
ideal price yields more per dollar than a new home does. The infill edge
activates at $70K/door (a 3–5 home dealer relationship) where 12.6% beats
anything purchasable, or wherever a pad is already owned and sitting empty.

## Exit value: this is a cash-flow play, not an appraisal play

At 10/11/12% exit caps a new home appraises at $87K / $79K / $72K vs $80K
cost — roughly break-even. The return is the decade of 11–13% unlevered yield,
not a value pop. Consistent with the asset-class rule: park-owned homes are
depreciating assets; returns must come from cash flow.

## Financing reality

Chattel-style debt (75% at 8.5%/15yr) eats it: 8.0% cash-on-cash — worse than
unlevered. Fund infill homes from park cash flow or a park-level refi, never
home-by-home chattel loans.

## STRATEGIC CONSEQUENCE — buy-box change

**Vacant pads are embedded development pipeline.** A park with utilities-live
vacant pads carries hidden units at 11–14% yield-on-cost that the seller's NOI
(and therefore the price) gives you for free. Screening preference, in order:
1. Parks with vacant, utilities-live pads (free option on 12%+ infill)
2. Parks with dying/oldest homes on good pads (replacement at turnover)
3. Full parks (Eden: 18/18 occupied — no infill option; its edge is carry + price)

Raw pad build-out (sewer tap, pad, pedestal: $15–25K extra) pushes cost to
$95–105K/door and kills the yield — "vacant pad" only counts with utilities in.

## Caveats before any home order

Wind-zone setup compliance (HUD Zone II here), pad rights/grandfathering in
writing, dealer setup quote incl. steps/skirting/tie-downs/delivery, insurance
add per home, 60–90 day delivery+set+lease timeline.
