## Asset-class risks (researched 2026-08-04) — what kills park-owned-home MHP deals
1. **Sewer catastrophe**: lagoon/pond systems are the highest-risk sewer type;
   sludge removal $20-50K+; DEQ non-compliance can threaten the park's viability.
2. **Bank refusal on park-owned homes**: lenders prefer tenant-owned (lot-rent)
   parks; park-owned homes read as management-heavy chattel.
3. **Homes are depreciating assets**: unlike apartments, the structures lose value;
   returns must come from cash flow (our model already assumes zero appreciation).
4. **Title defects**: missing/liened home titles surface at closing; each untitled
   home is salvage value.
5. **Turn cost blowouts**: an MH turn (floors, plumbing) runs multiples of an
   apartment turn; verify real per-turn cost history from seller.
6. **Zoning extinction**: many parishes won't permit new/rebuilt pads; grandfathered
   status must be confirmed in writing (also the moat — no new supply).
7. **Exit illiquidity**: buyer pool for park-owned-home parks is thin; assume the
   exit is another cash-flow buyer at a high cap (model already does: ~12% exit cap).
