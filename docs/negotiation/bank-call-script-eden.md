Goal of every call: ONE yes/no answer — "will you lend on an 18-pad MHP where the
park owns all 18 homes?" — plus terms if yes. 10 minutes per call. Ask for a
commercial lender, not the branch teller.

## The opening (verbatim)

"Morning — my name is Anthony Stuart, Ridgeback Peak Properties, here in Baton
Rouge. I'm under review on a stabilized income property in Denham Springs and
I'm looking for the right lending partner. Quick profile question before I take
your time: does the bank lend on mobile home communities where the park owns
the homes? This one is 18 homes on ~5 acres, fifteen of them 2017–2019 builds,
city water, seller's actual 2025 financials in hand, about $143K NOI. Purchase
price will be in the $1.2–1.4M range."

If NO → "Understood — does anyone at the bank do land-only loans on parks, and
would you point me to who in town writes park-owned communities?" (Their referral
is intel.) Thank, end, log.

If YES → the eight questions:

1. Max LTV on this asset type, and amortization? (House model: 70–75% / 20–25 yr)
2. Rate today for a first-time sponsor — fixed period and reset structure?
3. Do you size on DSCR? What minimum, on what vacancy assumption? (Ours: 1.30 at
   the seller's actual 13.7% loss — say it proudly, it's conservative.)
4. Will you allow a SUBORDINATED seller note behind you, to ~85% combined LTV,
   with a standstill? (This is the deal structure — disqualifying if no.)
5. Do the homes count in your collateral value, or land/improvements only? Do
   you require titles immobilized (converted to real property)?
6. Recourse: personal guaranty expected? (Assume yes at this size — confirm.)
7. Deposits: do you require the operating account? (Fine — relationship builder.)
8. Timeline: application to close, and what third-party reports do you order
   (appraisal type — income approach with home value? environmental?)

## Questions THEY will ask — prepared answers

- **"Tell me about your experience."** → "I spent [X years] at Stoa Group, the
  Baton Rouge multifamily developer — 2,000 units built and operated across the
  Gulf Coast. I've since built a full underwriting practice; this deal is
  underwritten on the seller's actual P&L with a 1.30 DSCR standard at actual
  vacancy. Third-party management will run day-to-day." (Don't overstate the
  role; the underwriting quality is the credibility.)
- **"What's your liquidity / net worth?"** → "$300K liquid committed to this
  acquisition between my equity and my primary investor — my LP funds 90%. I'll
  provide a personal financial statement; the credit package is ready."
  (docs/templates/credit-package.md — have it filled before calling.)
- **"What's the deal?"** → One breath: "18-pad park, Denham Springs, all
  park-owned homes, 15 of 18 are 2017–2019 builds. 2025 actuals: $220K
  collections, $143.5K NOI. At my target price that's an 8.4–11% going-in cap,
  bank DSCR ~1.9–2.0 at 70% LTV. Flood Zone X. Permitted oxidation pond with a
  maintenance contract, city water."
- **"Why is the seller selling / carrying?"** → "Local operator consolidating;
  the carry conversation is about bridging his price to my basis — you'd be in
  first position, note subordinated with a standstill."
- **"Occupancy?"** → "Physical occupancy is high; I underwrite the actual 13.7%
  economic loss from their books — I don't use pro-forma."
- **"Entity?"** → "Single-purpose LLC, Louisiana — Ridgeback Peak Properties.
  Formation finalizing now." (geauxBIZ agent acceptance MUST be done first.)

## Log after each call
Bank / person / date / POH answer / LTV / rate / am / DSCR floor / seller-note
answer / homes-in-collateral answer / next step. Add to docs/lender-matrix.md.

## Red flags from the bank side
- "Land value only" → loan shrinks to ~$400-500K, structure dies unless seller
  carries 40%+.
- "No subordinate debt" → carry path dies at that bank, keep calling.
- Rate >8.5% or am <20yr → run pymodel before accepting; stress grid passed
  7.5%/20 but not tested worse.
