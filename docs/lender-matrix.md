## Typical 2026 Terms — Small-Balance Commercial Multifamily (Community Bank/CU)

*General market knowledge for CRE loans under ~$3M in Louisiana. Not institution-specific
quotes. Verify every number with each lender.*

| Term | Typical range |
|------|---------------|
| **Rate basis** | Prime + 0.50%–1.75% (floating or reset) or 5-yr Treasury + 2.00%–2.75% (fixed). LA commercial mortgage rates reported ~5.17%–10.00% as of Jan 2026 (selectcommercial.com); expect mid-to-high single digits for a first-time sponsor on a stabilized asset. WSJ Prime was 6.75% as of Dec 2025 [VERIFY current before quoting]. |
| **Amortization** | 20–25 years. 25-yr am increasingly common for qualified borrowers. |
| **Term structure** | 5-yr balloon or 5-yr rate reset most common; some offer 7- or 10-yr fixed over 20–25-yr am. 15+ yr fixed is rare at community banks. |
| **DSCR minimum** | 1.25x standard; some banks 1.20x on strong deals, some hold a 1.30x internal floor. Ask how they calculate it (see questions below). |
| **LTV** | 75% standard; 80% possible with stronger sponsor or cross-collateral. Plan on 25% down. |
| **Recourse** | Full personal guarantee is standard for a first-time sponsor. Non-recourse / carve-out-only guarantees are generally not available until 2–3 closed deals. Guarantee burn-off after 24–36 months of clean payments is occasionally negotiable — ask. |
| **Deposit relationship** | Most community banks expect the operating account (and often treasury/business checking) to move to them as a loan condition. |
| **Appraisal** | 3–6 weeks after order. Budget it into the closing timeline. |
| **Closing timeline** | 45–75 days from term sheet. |
| **Mobile home parks** | Many community banks treat MHPs as a separate (sometimes disfavored) asset class — confirm appetite explicitly before sending an MHP deal. [General knowledge] |

## Call-Prep Script — First-Time Sponsor to Commercial Lender

**Ask for:** the commercial lending officer (banks) or business lending department (credit
unions). If routed to retail, say "commercial real estate loan for an investment property."

### 1. Intro (15 seconds)
> "My name is [NAME], I run Ridgeback Peak Properties, a Baton Rouge LLC acquiring small
> multifamily properties in the region. I previously worked at Stoa Group, the Gulf Coast
> multifamily developer. I'm building a lender bench before my first acquisition and
> wanted to see if this fits your commercial lending box."

### 2. Deal box (20 seconds)
> "I'm buying stabilized 4-to-30-unit properties and small mobile home parks in the
> Baton Rouge parishes, $400K to $1.5M purchase price, existing income-producing assets
> — no construction. I underwrite to a 1.30 debt service coverage internally, above the
> typical 1.25 minimum, and every deal I bring will come with a full underwriting
> package: rent roll analysis, normalized T-12, DSCR-constrained loan sizing, and
> sensitivity tables."

### 3. Capital position (10 seconds)
> "I have roughly $300K in committed equity — cash, primarily through a family limited
> partnership — so I'm putting 25% down plus closing costs and reserves. I can provide a
> personal financial statement and proof of funds."

### 4. The ask
> "I'm not asking for a commitment today. I'd like indicative terms so I can underwrite
> accurately: what rate basis, amortization, and structure would you quote on a deal
> like that for a first-time sponsor, and what would you need from me at application?"

Then ask the eight questions below. Take notes; log answers in this file.

### The 8 questions to ask every lender
1. **Rate basis:** What index do you price off (Prime, 5-yr Treasury, FHLB), what spread
   for a first-time sponsor, and is it fixed or floating?
2. **Amortization:** Standard am on small multifamily — 20 or 25 years? Anything longer?
3. **Balloon/reset:** Term structure — 5-yr balloon, 5-yr reset, or longer fixed? What
   happens at maturity (auto-renewal, re-underwrite, fees)?
4. **Recourse:** Full personal guarantee required? Any burn-off after seasoning? At what
   point (deals closed / track record) does carve-out-only become possible?
5. **DSCR calculation:** What minimum DSCR, and how do you calculate it — do you deduct
   replacement reserves, a management fee, and a vacancy factor from NOI, or use my
   in-place numbers?
6. **Deposit relationship:** Do you require operating accounts or other deposits with
   the bank as a loan condition? How much does it move pricing?
7. **Closing timeline:** From signed term sheet to funding, how many days? What are the
   usual bottlenecks?
8. **Appraisal:** Who orders it, what does it cost on a $400K–$1.5M multifamily, and
   what's current turnaround?

**After each call, record:** lender, contact name/direct line, date, answers to all 8,
and a gut read on appetite (hot / warm / cold) — in a `## Call log` section appended
to this file.
