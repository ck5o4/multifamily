Every future deal enters the system through this layer, so these are the
highest-leverage findings in the sweep. All three were verified by writing
through `safe_writer` into a copy of the master and **recalculating**, so the

the result carried **0 formula errors and 12/12 checks OK**.

**1. A T-12 line whose amount parses to `0` is written as a real $0 expense,
suppresses the derived default, and gets no flag at all.** `Insurance,0` matches
the category, is written as `$0/unit`, and marks insurance as *taken from the
deal's own documents* — so `defaults.derive`'s $2,000 inland / $3,000 wind value
never fires. `compare_to_benchmarks` gated its deviation test on `pu > 0`, so
**the single most extreme value possible printed with a blank deviation and a
blank flag.** On a 12-unit Gretna deal (Jefferson = $3,000/unit wind) at
$950,000:

| | insurance `0` written | line absent (default fires) |
|---|---|---|
| NOI yr1 | $92,546 | $56,546 |
| going-in cap | **9.74%** | 5.95% |
| equity | $263,625 | $449,616 |
| **levered IRR** | **+14.97%** | **+1.45%** |

ΔNOI $36,000/yr (= 12 × $3,000 exactly), **ΔIRR 13.52 points**, equity
understated $185,991 — and the verdict crosses from FAIL to above the 13%
pursue floor. Reachable on ordinary statements: insurance escrowed inside debt
service, carried at portfolio level, or prepaid outside the T-12 window. Literal
zeros are real in this repo's own files (hwy42 `City Water, 0`; eden's
`Water Credit Car Wash` Jul–Dec). The same hole swallowed a **negative** line.
**Fixed:** zero and negative per-unit lines now carry a `ZERO` / `NEGATIVE`
flag. `tools/parsers.py:compare_to_benchmarks`.

**2. An interior or trailing `Total …` row is mistaken for the annual-totals

`_find_total_col` kills everything *above* instead. On a Yardi/AppFolio

| NOI yr1 | $64,999 | $116,559 |
| going-in cap | 5.42% | **9.71%** |

| **levered IRR** | **−1.45%** | **+13.05%** |

**ΔIRR +14.50 points**, equity understated **$293,973** — against ~$300K of

`--apply`** when a T-12 was supplied and zero lines parsed, which blocks the
14.50-point error. The `_find_total_col` defect itself is **deferred** — picking
the right header row on an arbitrary statement layout is a parser redesign, not
a sweep patch, and the mid-statement-subtotal variant still loses rows silently.
