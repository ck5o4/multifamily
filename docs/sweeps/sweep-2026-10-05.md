### Monte Carlo

**F6. `_CHOL_L` row 5 did not reproduce the declared correlation matrix.** HIGH.
`tools/pymodel.py`. `L[5] = [-0.3, 0, 0, 0, 0, sqrt(0.91)]` satisfies
`rho(rg, turnover) = -0.3` and the unit norm but is not the Cholesky factor, so
it leaked `rho(expense_growth, turnover) = -0.090` and
`rho(vacancy, turnover) = +0.150` where the spec declares both 0.000 (realized
−0.0926 / +0.1480 on 100k draws). The comment above it read *"Verified: L @ L.T
reproduces the correlation matrix exactly"* — false. My independent Cholesky of
the target matrix matches the replacement row to 10 dp, and the target is
positive definite (eigenvalues 0.344–1.656).
Impact is within MC noise: 12-seed mean **+0.02 to +0.67pp** of beats-index
against a per-seed sd of 0.8–1.9pp, and **no deterministic rung moves**.
**One thing to know when reading the board:** at seed 42 alone it moves eden's
$1,443,000 rung 48.24% → 50.16%, crossing the 50% line; the 12-seed mean is
49.65%, still below, and the page's own ±3pp coin-flip annotation fires either
way. Do not read that flip as signal.
`test_mc`'s correlation test could not catch this: it samples `_tri_icdf` where
the engine uses `_pw_icdf`, checks 3 of 15 pairs (neither broken one), and both
corrupted values sit inside its ±0.15 tolerance. The new assertion is on the
constant — `max |L@Lᵀ − R| < 1e-9`, plus lower-triangularity and unit row norms
— so it costs no draws and no tolerance can defeat it.
