## Workbook formula suspicions — RESOLVED after three sweeps of "unverifiable"

These were carried unverified since 2026-09-07 because `--recalc` needs
LibreOffice Calc, which this container lacks. That was the wrong constraint:
**reading formula strings with `openpyxl(data_only=False)` needs neither recalc

**Every one of these is LATENT.** All eight live acq workbooks are amort 25 /
hold 5 / IO 0 / refi 0, so none is reachable today and **no deal decision moves.**

- **W1 CONFIRMED, workbook-only — `Inputs!B52` lacks the `max(0, …)` floor that
  `pymodel.py:254` has.** On a negative year-1 NOI (−$10,000 at $1M/75%/6.75%/25yr/1.30):
  loan **−$92,779.70**, monthly payment −$641.03, equity inflated to $1,111,851.90,
  total debt service **−$7,692.31** (the loan pays you), and `D38` DSCR prints
  exactly **1.3000 — passing `Checks!B3`** because negative/negative is positive.
  `Checks!B2` Sources=Uses balances to **0.000000** because the error is
  algebraically consistent. Every guard passes on a deal that cannot be financed.
- **W2 CONFIRMED — `Annual CF!D34` charges phantom debt service after payoff.**
  `D32` (amortizing branch) = `12*pmt − (D31−D35)` and `D33` = `D31−D35`, so
  `D34 = D32+D33 = 12*pmt` **unconditionally** — the terms cancel. With
  `amort < hold` the workbook keeps charging a full year of payments on a zero
  balance. `pymodel` was fixed 2026-08-09 and `test_no_phantom_debt_service`
  covers it; the workbook is the only place the bug survives.
- **W2b `Checks!B6` is a TAUTOLOGY.** "Loan balance non-negative at exit" tests
  `INDEX(C35:N35,…) >= 0`, but `D35 = MAX(0, FV(...))` is `>= 0` by construction,
  so B6 can never fail. False assurance, and it does not catch W2.
- **W3 CONFIRMED — the IO test is not refi-aware.** `D32`/`D35` switch on
  `D$2<=Inputs!$B$36` with no refi term, while the rate and payment selectors
  *inside* both branches do test `AND(F$14>0, D$2>F$14)`. pymodel is correct
  (`(not post_refi) and yr <= io_years`). A genuine workbook↔Python divergence
  that `test_pymodel.py` cannot catch, because no deal sets `io_years` or
  `refi_year` — so the parity claim does not cover the io+refi quadrant.
- **W4 REFUTED as a silent failure — close it.** `N46/N47/N48` are blank and
  `N50/N51` hardcode `+0` for the sale terms, but `Inputs!B40`'s own label reads

  year-10 exit, by design. Not silent, guarded, intended.
- **W5 REFUTED as silent.** At `refi_year == hold_years` the D48 double-benefit
  mechanism is real (D31 only switches to the new loan at F14+1, so you take
  cash-out F27 and pay off only the old balance), but `Checks!B10` requires

## The vacancy cap, quantified — why the house rule exists

`pymodel.py:1560-1573` allows at most **one turnover per unit per year**
(Bernoulli over `range(n_units_int)`) and draws days-vacant triangular with
hi=120. So maximum physical vacancy is `120/365 = 32.877%` regardless of unit
count, plus frictional ≤2.0%:

| deal | process ceiling | P99 | P99.9 | max over 600,000 draws | P(vac ≥ 33.3%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| treme (8u) | 33.77% | 15.98% | 18.85% | 22.32% | **0.0000%** |
| baker (12u) | 33.77% | 14.60% | 16.87% | 20.47% | **0.0000%** |
| eden (18u) | 39.97% | 19.65% | 21.59% | 24.25% | **0.0000%** |

**baker-trails' own OM rent roll shows 33.3% ACTUAL physical vacancy**, and the MC
assigns that outcome probability **zero in 600,000 draws**. This is the hard
arithmetic behind CLAUDE.md's rule that the deterministic stress grid outranks MC
for vacancy risk. The MC is not wrong; it is out of range, and `board.py` carries
**no deterministic vacancy stress row at all** (deferred below).
