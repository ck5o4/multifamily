**C-a. The mlk-2119 rebuttal switched exit-cap basis without saying so.** The
10-05 deferred list says *"`board.py:95` mlk-2119 'it loses money' is false —

that figure comes from:

| exit cap | levered IRR | equity multiple |
|---|---|---|
| **0.075 — what the deal carries** | **−9.7946%** | **0.6155×** |
| 0.05533 — going-in 5.033% + 50bps | **+1.3347%** | **1.0658×** |

`+1.3347% / 1.0658×` is the 10-05 figure to four decimals: it is the
derived-default basis, not the deal's own. On the carried basis the board,
`deals.json` and the parity-verified engine agree to the dollar — Y1 NOI
$24,661.38, loan $228,807.51 (47% LTV, DSCR-capped), equity $273,280.57.

Both conventions are live in the repo, which is the real defect: `pymodel.py:952`
derives going-in + 50bps when `exit_cap` is unset (`defaults.py:46` documents
it) and uses the carried value when it is set. `solve.py:9` is explicit that the
re-derivation exists so a **solved** low price "never gets credit for selling at
a richer valuation than it bought at" — a floor against optimism. Applied at the
**asking** price it runs the other way. An 11.13pp swing in IRR on one deal from
an unlabelled convention switch.

**No deal decision turns on this** — mlk-2119 is DEAD on either basis (+1.33%
misses the 13% floor by 11.67pp; −9.79% loses money). What it protects is the
published number: acting on that deferred item would have rewritten accurate
board copy to say a money-losing deal merely misses the band. Now pinned by a

record.

**C-b. `rho(rent_growth, vacancy)` is −0.07 to −0.17 on the live deals, not
"~−0.42".** The 10-05 backlog queues restating the documented −0.50 as ~−0.42.
Measured on the live engine (n=20,000, seed 11; Spearman within 0.003 of
Pearson): treme −0.0749, covington −0.0717, weber −0.0764, baker −0.1140, eden

strong on every live deal.** Honest restatement: *declared −0.50; realized −0.07
to −0.17 at this portfolio's 5–48 units, asymptoting to −0.26.*

**C-c. The engine does not read `turnover_rate`, the age bands, or the
