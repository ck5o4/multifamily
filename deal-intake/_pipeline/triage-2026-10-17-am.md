has succeeded since 2026-08-17 (`marketwatch-no-structured-rents`), so this is
the best anchor available and it is one zip off, not on-parcel.

Read the ladders at that anchor:

- **Starboard at ~$1,030 rents: IRR at ask ≈ 3%.** The 13% clearing price is
  roughly **$844K–$1,051K against a $1,170,000 ask** — over-asked by 10–28%.

  **$1,097K–$1,366K against $1,425,000** — over-asked by 4–23%.

draw outright, and loses it illiquidly). Neither is a nonsense ask: Jade at
$1,100 rents is within 4% of clearing 13%, which is negotiating distance, not a
different planet.
