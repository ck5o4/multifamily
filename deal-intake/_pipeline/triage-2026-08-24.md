**2119 MLK Jr Blvd — in the box, dead on the numbers.**

The Crexi alert carried no price or unit count. Both were pulled from public
listing data: **$490,000, 2023 new-construction duplex, two 3BR/2BA units,
2,667 SF total, owner financing offered.** The alert headline —
*"High Demand Tourism Area - Great for a 1031 Exchange"* — is an STR pitch.
Per CLAUDE.md, STR income is never underwritten as lease income, and Orleans
regulates non-owner-occupied STR heavily. Underwritten as a term-lease duplex,
which is the only honest way to hold it.

Rent assumption **$2,000/unit/mo, ESTIMATED, not verified**. Grounding: Central
City average apartment rent runs ~$1,637 and 62% of the stock rents
$1,501-2,000; $2,000 is a premium to the neighborhood for the 2023 vintage. The
in-repo Orleans comp (Treme, 650 SF 1BR at $1,400 = $2.15/SF) scales to roughly
$1,870-2,130 for a 1,333 SF 3BR. So $2,000 is the generous end of credible, and
the deal still fails.

Insurance was set to **$3,000/unit** (Orleans wind, per CLAUDE.md). The intake
template defaults to $2,000 and does **not** auto-escalate for metro parishes —
it had to be overridden by hand. Worth fixing in `intake.py` so metro deals stop
requiring a manual edit.

Two independent kills:

1. **Return.** A negative levered IRR and a 0.62x multiple. Not "below the 13%
   pursue floor" — it loses money. The solver puts 13% at **$269,000, 45% below
   ask**, and even at that price P(beat the index) is only 36%.
2. **Capital.** $273,281 of equity is 91% of the ~$300K available, spent on
   **two doors**, leaving nothing against the $30-40K liquid reserve rule. This
   is the same equity kill as 613 Yvonne St and cannon-rd, but inverted: those
   were too big, this one is simply priced per-door like a home.

Cross-check: **central-city-2nd** (1711 Second St, 7u, $166K/u) was killed dead
on 2026-08-08 in this same neighborhood. This one asks $245K/unit — 48% more per
door for a smaller property. Consistent.

The $490K ask is retail pricing aimed at an owner-occupant or a 1031 buyer
parking basis, not at an income buyer. Owner financing does not rescue it: the
gap is a 5.03% going-in cap, and no debt structure fixes a cap rate.
